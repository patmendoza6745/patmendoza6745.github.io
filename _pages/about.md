I'm a **Member of Technical Staff** at [xAI](https://x.ai/) on the Post-Training team. I'm especially interested in AI alignment and interpretability, with a particular emphasis on AI safety.

## Background

I studied EECS and Applied Mathematics at UC Berkeley, where I researched speech-language models in the Berkeley Artificial Intelligence Research (BAIR) Lab with [Cheol Jun Cho](https://cheoljun95.github.io/), [Nicholas Lee](https://www.linkedin.com/in/nicholas-lee-74731916a/), and Professor [Gopala Anumanchipalli](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~gopala/), and worked on LLM safety with David Wagner's Research Group alongside [Zhanhao Hu](https://whothu.github.io/) and Professor [David Wagner](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~daw/).

When I'm not working, I enjoy soccer and spending time with my four cats.
